RetireSafe Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

1. Overview

RetireSafe is an Atlassian Forge app for Jira that helps administrators identify stale projects and review project-retirement readiness and dependencies.

2. Data RetireSafe accesses

To provide its functionality, RetireSafe may access Jira project and configuration metadata, project activity information, issue statistics, workflows, schemes, filters, project lead information, and related Jira data available through Atlassian APIs.

3. Data RetireSafe stores

RetireSafe uses Atlassian Forge hosted storage for persistent app data. Depending on the app workflow, stored data may include Jira project identifiers and keys, scan or review status, retirement-readiness metadata, timestamps, Atlassian account IDs associated with review activity, and reviewer-entered notes.

4. Data location

RetireSafe does not operate a separate external application database for Jira customer data. Persistent app data is stored using Atlassian Forge hosted storage and is subject to Atlassian's Forge hosting and data-residency capabilities.

5. Data sharing

RetireSafe does not sell End-User Data and does not use End-User Data for advertising. RetireSafe does not intentionally share Jira customer data with third-party advertising or analytics providers.

6. Data retention and deletion

Stored app data is retained only as needed to provide RetireSafe functionality. Customers may request assistance concerning RetireSafe data through the contact information published on the RetireSafe Atlassian Marketplace listing.

7. Security

RetireSafe is designed as a read-only analysis tool for Jira project-retirement review. It does not intentionally delete, archive, or modify Jira projects or Jira configuration as part of its analysis workflow.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as RetireSafe evolves. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact published on the RetireSafe Atlassian Marketplace listing.
